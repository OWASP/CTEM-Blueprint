# OWASP CTEM Blueprint — First-year Roadmap

**Status:** Working project plan based on the **10 October 2026 Q1 planning meeting**. Proposed deliverables remain subject to leader review and community feedback.

The project covers **web, API, mobile and AI/LLM security**. Quarter numbers refer to project milestones rather than fixed calendar quarters.

| Quarter | Theme | Planned outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Q1** | Framework foundation | README, open licenses, contribution/governance docs, scope, lifecycle, glossary, responsible AI and architecture diagrams |
| **Q2** | Taxonomy and mappings | Draft CTEM Top 10, mappings to existing OWASP web/API/mobile/GenAI and automated-threat resources, reviewed scenarios |
| **Q3** | Safe reference tooling | Proof-of-concept validation playbooks, CI/CD examples, edge/telemetry reference architecture; limited to approved test systems |
| **Q4** | Integration and community validation | Example dashboards and evidence workflows, industry scenarios, documentation refinement, review of candidate release |

See [Q1 checklist](docs/q1-completion-checklist.md). Do not mark items as complete until evidence exists.

## Q2 boundaries

The CTEM Top 10 will be a **draft proposal** for discussion and evidence gathering, **not an official OWASP Top 10** merely because it appears in this repository. Risk categories and mappings need community review and source references.

## Q3 safeguards

Reference tooling is intended for defensive validation in controlled environments. The project does not promise a production-grade Kasada equivalent or make any proprietary technology a dependency.

## Community and release practice

Aim for at least one meaningful public release per year, with a changelog and project-page activity updates. Avoid promising a stable release before security review, maintainership and usable artifacts are established.
